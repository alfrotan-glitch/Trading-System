## 2. What was falsified this series (do not re-tune)

- **Weekly frequency** (W1 + WHYB, 68 discovery configs): holdout
  Sharpe decays to 0.24–0.56, DSR ≈ 0. The mechanism is
  monthly-horizon-specific. Branch closed.
- **Fast ensembles as the primary signal**: more turns, worse
  walk-forward, worse DSR. Turnover is not the binding constraint —
  decade stability is.
- **USD-agreement (both sides gated)**: halves the evaluation return
  vs the asymmetric veto. The long side of gold trend wants no
  confirmation; the short side demands it. That asymmetry *is* the
  finding.
- Slow MA/breakout states at monthly frequency never reach the
  ≥20-turn eligibility (19 turns in 27 years) — unregistrable under
  the inherited selection rule, not merely unprofitable.

## 3. Why the gate chain still (correctly) refuses

G3 (≥6/10 positive WF years), G5 (DSR ≥ 0.95) and G6 (≥100 OOS turns)
jointly encode "frequent, decade-stable, selection-proof". The best
mechanism gold offers on 53 years of monthly data is infrequent
(~1 turn/year), decade-variable (1990s: −0.25…+0.27 SR), and strong
mostly in the post-2000 evaluation era. Reaching G5/G6 by trading
faster measurably destroys the Sharpe (ENS/W1 evidence). The gates
were not moved, averaged, or re-weighted; at N=262 cumulative trials
the deflation bar is doing exactly its job.

## 4. The one legitimate next step: forward validation

`ts9_lo` (primary) and `veto9` (robustness companion) should be frozen
**as-is** — parameters, sizing, USD basket, execution convention — and
run as paper/shadow forward observation on data that does not yet
exist in any research window (monthly closes from 2026-10 onward, plus
measured MT5 demo costs when the Windows terminal is available). Only
forward evidence can: (i) accumulate OOS turns toward G6 without
reusing the holdout, (ii) raise the DSR sample without raising N, and
(iii) replace ASSUMED with MEASURED costs, the standing blocker for
any economic claim in this repository. Expected decision-relevant
horizon: ~24–36 forward months minimum. Until then both remain
`RESEARCH`-stage candidates; the DEMO execution probe and all safety
state are unchanged.
