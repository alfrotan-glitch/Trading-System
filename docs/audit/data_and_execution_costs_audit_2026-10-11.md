A critical discrepancy exists between the MetaQuotes Python documentation and empirical MT5 behavior:

* **Documentation claim:** The MetaQuotes page for `copy_ticks_range` states that *"MetaTrader 5 stores tick and bar open time in UTC time zone (without the shift)... The data obtained from MetaTrader 5 have UTC time."* (`XAUUSD__730d_gap_disposition_verified.json`).

  2. **Historical `copy_ticks_range` archive (`XAUUSD__730d_gap_disposition_verified.json` & `reports/xauusd_temporal_discovery_state.json`):**
     - In `XAUUSD__730d_gap_disposition_verified.json`, the weekly Friday-to-Monday closures (`gap-0001` through `gap-0005`, Sept–Oct 2024, US EDT) begin at raw `time_msc` corresponding to **Friday `23:58:59`** (`2024-09-20T23:58:59.994Z` provisional) and end at **Monday `01:00:01`** (`2024-09-23T01:00:01.141Z` provisional) — a duration of `176,461s` (`49h 01m`).

## 4. Execution Cost Infrastructure & Measured vs. Assumed Parameters

### 4.1 Audit of Cost Capture & Reconciliation (`src/qts/execution/cost_capture.py`, `src/qts/research/costs.py`)

The cost infrastructure enforces strict provenance separation (`CostBasis.MEASURED` vs. `CostBasis.ASSUMED`, `CLAIM_ELIGIBLE_BASES = frozenset({CostBasis.MEASURED})`):

* **Pre-trade quote capture (`MT5Adapter._quote_snapshot`):** Captures `bid`, `ask`, and `spread_points` *before* `mt5.order_send` is called. If no tick is available, records `unavailable = True` and `spread_points = None` (never `0`).
* **Post-fill deal capture (`deal_evidence_from_broker`):** Reads `commission`, `swap`, `fee`, `price`, `volume`, `profit`, `time`, and `time_msc` from MT5 deal rows (`DEAL_ENTRY_IN`, `DEAL_ENTRY_OUT`, `DEAL_ENTRY_INOUT`).
  - Missing or `NaN` fields are added to `unavailable_fields` and set to `None` (`complete = False`), never defaulted to `Decimal("0")`.
  - `reported_cost` (`-(commission + swap + fee)`) is computed **only** when all three fee fields are present and finite.
  - Slippage (`price_Units`, `points`, `bps`, `account_ccy`) is computed **only** when requested price, fill price, side, and contract geometry are all present.
* **Tamper-evident persistence (`CostEvidenceStore`):** Append-only JSONL with SHA-256 hash chaining (`prev_hash` -> `entry_hash`) and a `.head` sidecar that detects tail truncation on `verify_chain()`.
* **Reconciliation (`reconcile_store`):** Pairs `IN` and `OUT` deals by `position_id`, computes actual overnight `nights_held` (`max(0, (exit_date - entry_date).days)` when both deal timestamps are `utc-corrected`), and compares observed round-trip cost against `CostModel.round_turn_Order_cost()`.
