# QTS Gold — deterministic reflection after every 10 complete trades

**2026-11-01 • Research only • LIVE LOCKED • No validated profitable edge**

## Trader's question

I am not interested in a high win rate if the net reward after real broker
fees and adverse stop fills is negative. For each actual broker transaction,
I want the *original chosen strategy*, contemporaneous market regime,
entry/stop/target, stop versus TP exit, costs, risk distance, and whether the
*observed executable Bid/Ask* ever touched the original target after the stop.
I refuse to guess a stopped trade would have been profitable with a wider SL.

The existing V4 native MT5 tester now produces:

- `QTS_V4_POSITION_ATTRIBUTION.json`: original broker order comment
  (`QTSV4-S8-R2`), reconciled entry/exit deal IDs, per-strategy PnL,
  measured costs, position timestamps and duration
- `QTS_V4_POST_STOP_STRATEGY_COHORTS.json`: strategy-specific stop replay
  with complete/unknown quote denominators; without matched post-stop
  Bid/Ask ticks **all future TP touch conclusions remain UNKNOWN**
- **NEW** `QTS_TEN_TRADE_REFLECTION.json`: complete position review and
  chronological 10-trade checkpoints generated by the same automatic runner

No command is needed after a normal V4 Strategy Tester run. For an existing
fully attributed native run, the same read-only report may be reproduced:

```powershell
python gold_robot/research/ten_trade_learning.py --attribution "QTS_V4_POSITION_ATTRIBUTION.json" --post-stop "QTS_V4_POST_STOP_STRATEGY_COHORTS.json" --output "QTS_TEN_TRADE_REFLECTION.json"
```

## Every trade and every ten closed positions

The learner sorts all finished broker positions by the actual exit timestamp
and unique broker position ID. Positions 1–10 form checkpoint 1, 11–20
checkpoint 2, etc. No rolling repeated windows that count one trade ten
times; a tail of fewer than 10 stays pending, while *every* finished trade
still receives its own descriptive diagnostic.

Individual records distinguish native SL/TP, an early stopped position,
stop-outs, high observed cost drag, target gain swallowed by **additional
hypothetical $10/lot stress**, missing synchronized post-stop quotes and
actual observed TP touch *after* a stopped trade. They do NOT claim causes
without quote paths, or that an observed after-stop move was a real win.

At each full 10-trade checkpoint, report:
- Realised broker net, stressed net, broker-defined SL/TP counts
- Stressed wins/losses, and **closed-deal** drawdown (not floating-equity DD)
- Actual native strategies and market regimes represented by that batch
- Adverse strategy/regime cohorts, with **cohort size and loss counts**
- Explicit next research action without any real/DEMO broker order change

## What a 'self correction' means at a 10-trade checkpoint

If a given style/regime has 3 or more trades in a checkpoint, at least
two **after-stress losing** positions and negative aggregate stressed net,
the system records a new **shadow-only research hypothesis**:

`SHADOW_ONLY_SKIP_STYLE_IN_SPECIFIC_REGIME`

It must not be mistaken for proof that not taking those trades would have
increased profits. The original 10 trades are not a valid test of this
candidate: selection, capital use and subsequent positions would differ.

If the same cohort has independently adverse outcomes in **two distinct,
non-overlapping 10-trade checkpoints**, it is flagged for an *unseen*
challenger experiment. Neither rule authorizes automatically disabling the
strategy or tweaking existing stop-loss/TP, risk percent, leverage or lot.
The original default behaviour and profit/risk gates stay unchanged.

**10 trades are enough to trigger structured diagnostics, not enough to
estimate a reliable conditional trading edge.** For independent acceptance,
this project retains the research thresholds: >=6 complete future monthly
windows, >=200 genuine position records, true broker costs, and independent
forward-style testing (these are necessary screening thresholds, **not**
sufficient to license REAL). Any changes to routing/SL/TP after seeing
results are new hypotheses with their own future holdout.

## Engineering/operating limitations

- Native MT5 real-tick compilation and actual WMMarkets broker fee/lot
  qualification are not guaranteed by CI source tests.
- You must export and match real post-exit Bid/Ask ticks for any claim that
  price hit the original TP after a STOP. No replacement with candle OHLC.
- Raw source hashes and original native broker records must remain available.
  A user-modified JSON is not an independent trusted broker statement.
- The automatic learner fails closed on corrupted timestamps, duplicate
  native deal IDs, incomplete source attribution, inconsistent stop
  replay or unsupported safety flags.
- The research tool never logs in, submits orders, changes input presets,
  recompiles an EA, rewrites strategies, increases lots, or promotes its
  own shadow suggestions to live configuration.
- No claim of profitability, zero risk, 100% accurate signals, or
  authorization of REAL trading. `LIVE LOCKED`.

The architecture now separates three stages:
**observe every trade → critique every 10 → propose frozen shadow alternatives**.
A future independent native tester implementation can evaluate the frozen
alternative against the unchanged V4 benchmark on *new data*, but must
never turn in-sample post-hoc profit into a deployment permission.
